FAQ

Before you order.

Answers about research-use policy, batch documentation, shipping, and support.

Are products for human use?

No. All products sold by Good Morning Peptides are strictly for in-vitro laboratory and research use only. They are not intended for human or veterinary consumption, injection, or any clinical application.

What purity level are your peptides and how is it verified?

Our peptides are tested to 99%+ purity via HPLC analysis. Every batch undergoes third-party analytical review and results are published as a Certificate of Analysis available on our COA page by lot number.

What is a Certificate of Analysis (COA) and how do I read it?

A COA is a document from an independent laboratory that confirms the identity, purity, and concentration of a research compound. It shows the test methods used, the results, and whether the batch meets spec. You can look up any supported lot on our COA page.

What comes free with every order?

Every order ships with a free 30mL vial of Bacteriostatic Water as a laboratory preparation supply, included at no extra charge.

How should research materials be stored?

Lyophilized peptides should be stored in a cool, dry environment away from direct light. Long-term storage in a freezer at -20°C is recommended. Prepared research materials should be handled according to internal laboratory protocols and applicable documentation.

How long is the lyophilized product stable?

Properly stored lyophilized peptides are generally stable for 24 months or longer. Refer to lot documentation and institutional protocols for prepared material handling timelines.

How fast do you ship?

Orders placed before 1PM local time typically ship same business day. All orders include tracking and are packed for secure transit.

Do you ship internationally?

At this time we ship within the United States only. International shipping is not currently available.

Can I verify a lot number?

Yes. Visit our COA Lookup page and enter your lot number to pull the corresponding Certificate of Analysis for your batch.

What is your return and refund policy?

Due to the nature of research materials we do not accept returns. If your order arrives damaged or incorrect please contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we will make it right.
