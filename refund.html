Refund Policy

Policy for research materials, damaged orders, incorrect items, and eligible order issues.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

Due to the nature of research materials, all sales are generally final once an order has shipped. This policy explains when replacements, refunds, store credits, or other resolutions may be considered.

2. Final Sale Research Materials

Products sold by Good Morning Peptides are research-use-only materials. For quality, documentation, safety, and chain-of-custody reasons, general returns of shipped products are not accepted.

3. Eligible Issues

Good Morning Peptides may review requests involving incorrect items received, missing items, damaged items, damaged packages, fulfillment errors, duplicate orders caused by a technical issue, or orders canceled before fulfillment begins.

4. Reporting Window

Order issues must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Requests after 48 hours may be denied.

5. Required Information

To review a request, customers must provide the order number, full name and contact email, description of the issue, photos of package and product condition, and photos of the packing slip or label when applicable.

6. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds or replacements may not be available for buyer's remorse, incorrect addresses supplied by the customer, packages marked delivered by the carrier, refusal of delivery, failure to retrieve a package, opened or altered products, improperly stored or handled products, carrier, weather, or holiday delays, or orders placed in violation of the Terms of Service.

7. Cancellations

Orders may be canceled only before fulfillment begins. Once an order has been packed, labeled, or shipped, cancellation may no longer be available.

8. Refund Method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method when possible. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider or bank.

9. Replacements and Store Credit

Good Morning Peptides may offer replacement or store credit at its discretion based on the order issue and supporting documentation.

10. Chargebacks

Customers should contact support first so eligible issues can be reviewed. Chargebacks submitted without first contacting support may delay resolution.

11. Contact

Refund questions may be sent to support@goodmorningpeptides.com.
