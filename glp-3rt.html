Research overview

GLP-3RT is a synthetic 39-amino-acid acylated research peptide supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research. It is a structural analog of retatrutide (LY3437943), a unimolecular agonist engineered to bind the GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon (GCGR) receptors. Each lot is supplied as a lyophilized powder with batch-specific Certificate of Analysis documentation confirming identity and purity. Not for human or veterinary use.
