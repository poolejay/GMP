Privacy Policy

How Good Morning Peptides collects, uses, protects, and shares information.

Last updated: June 2026
OverviewInformation CollectedPaymentsUse of InformationSharingYour RightsContact

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies when users visit Good Morning Peptides, browse the catalog, complete researcher verification, place orders, contact support, or otherwise interact with the website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect name, email address, phone number, billing address, shipping address, order information, account information if accounts are enabled, cart or wishlist activity, researcher verification confirmations, customer support communications, IP address, browser and device information, referral pages, pages viewed, cookies and analytics data, and payment status or transaction metadata. Good Morning Peptides does not intend to store full payment card numbers on its own servers.

3. Information We Do Not Intend to Collect

Good Morning Peptides is not a healthcare provider and does not intend to collect medical histories, diagnosis information, treatment information, dosing information, personal-use information, or health records. Users should not submit medical, dosing, injection, treatment, or personal-use information through the site or support channels.

4. Payment Information

Payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers. Good Morning Peptides does not intend to store full payment card numbers on its own servers.

5. WooCommerce / Ecommerce Platform Data

If our checkout is powered by WooCommerce or another ecommerce platform, that platform may collect and store information needed for cart functionality, order processing, account login, checkout, tax records, and purchase history. Payment processing may be handled by secure third-party gateways.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to operate the website, process and fulfill orders, verify research-use acknowledgments, provide customer support, send order confirmations and shipping updates, maintain COA and documentation tools, improve site functionality, prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions, comply with legal, tax, accounting, and business obligations, and send marketing emails only if users opt in or where permitted.

7. Email Marketing

If users subscribe or purchase, Good Morning Peptides may send order-related emails and, where permitted, product updates or promotional emails. Users may unsubscribe from promotional emails, but may still receive transactional or order-related emails.

8. SMS Marketing

We do not currently use SMS marketing. If SMS messaging is enabled in the future, this Privacy Policy should be updated before launch of that feature.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may support cart functionality, login sessions if enabled, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and site performance. If advertising pixels or retargeting tools are added later, this policy should be updated.

10. Sharing of Information

We may share information with payment processors, shipping carriers, hosting providers, ecommerce platform providers, email and support providers, analytics and security providers, legal or regulatory authorities when required, and corporate successors in a sale, merger, or reorganization. We do not sell personal information for money. If advertising pixels or retargeting tools are enabled later, some state privacy laws may treat certain advertising disclosures as sharing and this policy should be updated accordingly.

11. Data Retention

We retain information as long as reasonably necessary for orders, tax and accounting records, legal obligations, fraud prevention, disputes, security, and business operations.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

13. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on location and applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request information about personal information. Requests can be sent to support@goodmorningpeptides.com.

14. California Privacy Notice

California residents may have rights under applicable privacy laws to know, access, correct, delete, opt out of certain sharing or sales, and not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights. Requests may be sent to support@goodmorningpeptides.com.

15. International Users

The site is operated from the United States. If users access the site from outside the United States, their information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States.

16. Children's Privacy

The site is intended for users 21 and older. Good Morning Peptides does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 21.

17. Third-Party Links

Third-party websites and services have their own privacy policies. Good Morning Peptides is not responsible for those independent practices.

18. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued site use after an update means the revised policy applies.

19. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to support@goodmorningpeptides.com.
