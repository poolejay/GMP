Research overview

GHK-Cu is a copper-binding tripeptide research complex composed of glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine (Gly-His-Lys) chelated to a copper(II) ion, supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research. GHK is an endogenous tripeptide identified in human plasma, saliva, and urine. Each lot is supplied as a lyophilized powder with batch-specific Certificate of Analysis documentation confirming identity and purity. Not for human or veterinary use.
