Research overview

GLP-2TZ is a synthetic 39-amino-acid acylated research peptide supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research. It is a structural analog of tirzepatide (LY3298176), a dual agonist engineered to bind the GLP-1 and GIP receptors, built on a GIP peptide backbone with a C20 fatty diacid side chain. Each lot is supplied as a lyophilized powder with batch-specific Certificate of Analysis documentation confirming identity and purity. Not for human or veterinary use.
